# Publication Risk Review

Read this reference for risk review, rejected-chapter rewriting, title review,
sensitive-material revision, or copyright-risk reduction. Improve
publishability without claiming that any platform will approve a submission.

## Risk Levels

- `low`: no obvious issue; normal editorial polish may still help.
- `medium`: content may create review ambiguity, reader discomfort, or avoidable legal risk; revise before publishing.
- `high`: rewrite the relevant plot, relationship, setting, or presentation.
- `cannot assist`: the user asks to preserve a prohibited core, evade review,
  conceal meaning, create explicit sexual content, sexualize minors, provide
  graphic abuse, or closely copy protected material.

## Review Dimensions

### Sexual Or Suggestive Material

Check for explicit detail, sexualized presentation, coercion, exploitative relationships, sexualized minors, or intimacy used mainly as a provocative selling point.

Rewrite toward emotional exchange, trust, restraint, adult relationships, consent, relationship decisions, fade-to-black transitions, or non-sexual conflict.

### Minors

Check whether a character is under 18 or ambiguously young. Do not sexualize
minors or provide intimate detail. Do not glorify harmful conduct such as
bullying, smoking, drinking, or gang involvement.

Rewrite toward friendship, family communication, learning, competition, mystery, adventure, self-development, consequences, or an adult time skip where appropriate.

### Violence And Distress

Check for graphic wounds, organs, torture detail, prolonged cruelty, or violence presented as spectacle.

Rewrite toward concise action, aftermath, emotional impact, escape, accountability, lawful intervention, or recovery.

### Sensitive Real-World Material

Check for attacks on real groups, extremist promotion, harmful ideological recruitment, illegal conduct promotion, or direct real-world inflammatory targeting.

Rewrite toward fictional organizations, commercial conflict, family conflict,
fictional-city events, or non-targeted character-level stakes when appropriate.
Do not use fictionalization to preserve harmful advocacy.

### Copyright And Imitation

Check for copied lyrics, real celebrity fan-fiction intimacy, recognizable
protected characters, proprietary names, signature world rules, copied scenes,
or close imitation of a named living author's style.

Rewrite toward original names, institutions, competitions, professions,
systems, relationships, settings, and scene logic. Summarize or replace
copyrighted lyrics rather than quoting them.

### Titles And Metadata

Check titles, synopsis text, and chapter names for crude phrasing, sexual suggestion, graphic violence, sensitive targeting, superstition promotion, or protected IP terms.

Rewrite toward character goals, suspense, conflict, growth, decisions, reversals, or fate changes.

## Refusal Language

Match the user's language when refusing or redirecting.

Chinese pattern:

```text
我不能帮助保留违规核心或规避审核。可以把这段改成以人物后果、关系变化、情节转场或非露骨冲突为核心的版本。
```

English pattern:

```text
I can't help preserve the risky core or provide a way to evade review. I can
help rewrite the scene around character consequence, relationship change,
narrative transition, or a non-explicit conflict that serves the same plot
function.
```

Keep refusals brief. Do not lecture. Offer a safe rewrite direction when possible.
