# Publication Risk Review

Read this reference for risk review, rejected-chapter rewriting, title review, sensitive-material revision, or copyright-risk reduction. Improve publishability without claiming that any platform will approve a submission.

## Risk Levels

- `low`: no obvious issue; normal editorial polish may still help.
- `medium`: content may create review ambiguity, reader discomfort, or avoidable legal risk; revise before publishing.
- `high`: rewrite the relevant plot, relationship, setting, or presentation.
- `cannot assist`: the user asks to preserve a prohibited core, evade review, conceal meaning, create explicit sexual content, sexualize minors, provide graphic abuse, or closely copy protected material.

## Review Dimensions

### Sexual Or Suggestive Material

Check for explicit detail, sexualized presentation, coercion, exploitative relationships, sexualized minors, or intimacy used mainly as a provocative selling point.

Rewrite toward emotional exchange, trust, restraint, adult relationships, consent, relationship decisions, fade-to-black transitions, or non-sexual conflict.

### Minors

Check whether a character is under 18 or ambiguously young. Do not sexualize minors or provide intimate detail. Do not glorify harmful conduct such as bullying, smoking, drinking, or gang involvement.

Rewrite toward friendship, family communication, learning, competition, mystery, adventure, self-development, consequences, or an adult time skip where appropriate.

### Violence And Distress

Check for graphic wounds, organs, torture detail, prolonged cruelty, or violence presented as spectacle.

Rewrite toward concise action, aftermath, emotional impact, escape, accountability, lawful intervention, or recovery.

### Sensitive Real-World Material

Check for attacks on real groups, extremist promotion, harmful ideological recruitment, illegal conduct promotion, or direct real-world inflammatory targeting.

Rewrite toward fictional organizations, commercial conflict, family conflict, fictional-city events, or non-targeted character-level stakes when appropriate. Do not use fictionalization to preserve harmful advocacy.

### Copyright And Imitation

Check for copied lyrics, real celebrity fan-fiction intimacy, recognizable protected characters, proprietary names, signature world rules, copied scenes, or close imitation of a named living author's style.

Rewrite toward original names, institutions, competitions, professions, systems, relationships, settings, and scene logic. Summarize or replace copyrighted lyrics rather than quoting them.

### Titles And Metadata

Check titles, synopsis text, and chapter names for crude phrasing, sexual suggestion, graphic violence, sensitive targeting, superstition promotion, or protected IP terms.

Rewrite toward character goals, suspense, conflict, growth, decisions, reversals, or fate changes.

## Rewrite Method

1. Identify the actual risky core.
2. Decide whether to delete, soften, age up, fictionalize, change the relationship, change the stakes, or replace the scene.
3. Rewrite the scene so the new plot still advances.
4. Check that the rewrite remains readable and does not preserve the old meaning through coded wording.
5. State any remaining author decision briefly.

## Refusal Pattern

Keep refusals short:

```text
我不能帮助保留违规核心或规避审核。可以把这段改成不依赖露骨细节的关系推进、冲突升级或转场留白，并保留本章需要的剧情结果。
```
